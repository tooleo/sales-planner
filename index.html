Sales Planner

Enter an hour

Open the Store Hourly KPIs report and use the bottom half of the page (the top half is shifted and doesn't line up). Pick the hour below, then find that row (like 12 PM) and type the numbers under TY (this year).

What to write on the paper

Same layout as the planner chart. Write each box in rows 4 to 7. The yellow column is the hour you just finished. Tap a column heading to edit that hour.

What's going on

Optional. Fill these in (from the top of the planner) to get a one-line summary.

Weekly Sales Progress

Use the DAY/WTD/MTD report dated the day before today's planner. Only read the WTD columns (the middle group).

Check against the report's Total row

At the bottom of the Store Hourly KPIs report, find the Total row and type its numbers.

Hourly reminders

Phone web pages can't reliably buzz you when closed, so this makes a calendar file. Open it and add it to your phone's calendar: you'll get an alert at 5 minutes past every hour from 11:05 AM to 9:05 PM, every day.

To stop them, delete the "Sales Planner" events from your calendar.
